Who I Am

I'm a B.Tech student passionate about applied intelligence — the kind that moves, perceives, and reasons. I spend my time building systems where hardware meets algorithms: robotic bot that navigates and extinguish fire, vision pipelines that differentiates between very similar objects, and language models that assist.

My work spans the full stack of modern AI: from designing embedded control loops in Robotics, training neural nets for Image Processing, fine-tuning Large Language Models, to building ML pipelines that scale.

Outside of code, I am a UI/UX Designer who enjoys collaborate with various users,connect to them and deliver elegant solutions to messy real-world design problems by prototyping and redesigning.
